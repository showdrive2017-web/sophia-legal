SOPHIA Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Yoshinori Kusakari (草苅佳宣; "we") sets out below how user information is handled in the story-building app "SOPHIA" ("the App").

1. Information we collect

Your own sky (used on your own)

Your work (star titles, text, connections, notes and embedded images) is stored only on your device. We do not send it to our servers or to any third party.

Same Sky (inviting others / being invited)

A sky you use together with others is stored on servers we use, not on your device. If you do not use this feature, nothing is sent to the servers. We store the following.

What we storeWhy
An automatically created user number (not linked to your name or email address)To tell whose star is whose, so that only you can edit your own stars
The name you enter and the colour you chooseTo show the other members of the sky whose star it is
The stars you write in Same Sky (title, text, date, time), their positions, and linesTo show the same sky on every member's screen
Whether you have the sky open right now, when you joined, and the invite code you used to joinTo show "online" status and to manage invitations
A record of invite-code lookups (the code and the time)To prevent invite codes from being guessed by trying every combination
A record that your purchase was verified (the store's purchase number, or a code derived from the proof of purchase; no payment information such as card numbers)To check whether you can use the full-version features (inviting friends, making four or more stars)
Report records (a copy of the reported star or name, the reporter's user number, the reason)To review reports and act on them
  • What you write in Same Sky is visible to every member of that sky (up to 6 people).
  • We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts or advertising IDs. No login is required.
  • The servers are Google LLC's Firebase (Realtime Database in Singapore; user-number management in the United States; purchase verification, reports and deletion are processed in Japan). When you connect, your IP address and similar information are processed on Google's infrastructure (Google Privacy Policy).
  • We may look at the contents of Same Sky only when we receive a report, when investigating a bug, or when required by law. We do not use the contents for advertising or analytics.

Overall summary feature (PC version only)

Using an API key that you provide yourself, this feature sends the names and contents of your stars to Anthropic (the company that provides Claude) and receives a summary. It works only when you choose to use it; if you do not use it, nothing is sent. Your API key is stored only in the browser on your device.

2. Advertising

The App does not show ads.

3. In-app purchase

The App offers a one-time purchase that removes the limit on the number of stars. The price is shown on the store's purchase screen. Purchases are handled by Google Play / the Apple App Store, and payment information such as credit card numbers is never passed to us. The only thing we receive is whether or not you have purchased.

For Same Sky, to check whether you have the full version, we send the proof of purchase issued by the store to our server and verify it with Apple or Google. As before, payment information such as card numbers is never passed to us.

4. Tracking for analytics and advertising

The App does not include any analytics tools (such as usage tracking) that collect statistics on how it is used.

However, the App's official website (sophia-universe.com) uses Google LLC's measurement tag (Google tag) to measure the effectiveness of ads. When you visit the site, information such as which pages you viewed and whether you clicked links to the App Store, Google Play or the PC download is sent to Google through cookies and similar technologies. We use this information to count which ads led people to get the App. Your name and email address are not sent. This measurement is not done inside the App.

  • Recipient: Google LLC (Google Privacy Policy)
  • How to stop it: turn off ad personalisation in Google's ad settings, or disable cookies in your browser.
  • For visits from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, advertising cookies are not used without consent.

The official website also uses Cloudflare, Inc.'s web analytics (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to count page views. Information such as the pages viewed, the referring page and the browser type is sent to Cloudflare. Your name and email address are not sent. This measurement is not done inside the App either.

  • Recipient: Cloudflare, Inc. (Cloudflare Privacy Policy)

5. Children's privacy

The App is not made mainly for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. Same Sky is for people aged 13 and over.

6. Deleting your data

  • Your own sky (used on your own): it exists only on your device, so you can erase it at any time with "Delete" in the App or by uninstalling the App.
  • Same Sky:
    • You can delete your own stars yourself at any time.
    • With "Delete my Same Sky data" in the App's settings, you can erase the stars you wrote, your name, your colour, your participation records, the record that your purchase was verified, and your user number from the servers all at once. A sky you host is deleted as a whole.
    • Even after you have deleted the App, you can make a request through the data deletion page.
    • A sky that no one opens any more is automatically deleted from the servers 180 days after it was last opened.
    • Report records may be kept so that we can act on them.
    • We cannot delete copies or screenshots that other people have already saved.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as features are added. If there is an important change, the update date will be shown at the top of this page.
