SOPHIA "Same Sky" Terms of Use

Last updated: November 13, 2026

These terms are the rules for using SOPHIA's "Same Sky" (a feature for inviting others and using one sky together; "the Feature"). By starting to use the Feature, you agree to these terms. The operator is Yoshinori Kusakari (草苅佳宣; "we").

1. Who can use it

  • The Feature can be used by people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.
  • Only people who have bought the full version can invite others. People who are invited can join for free.

2. What you write in a sky

  • The content of the stars you write belongs to you. We handle it only to show it on other members' screens and to store it.
  • What you write in Same Sky is visible to every member of that sky. Do not write anything you would not want seen, or other people's personal information.
  • Keep your own copy of anything important. The Feature does not guarantee that content will be kept.

3. What you must not do

Writing the following, or doing the following, is prohibited.

  • Content that hurts, threatens, harasses or discriminates against people
  • Sexual content, violent content, or content that encourages harming yourself or others
  • Content that breaks the law or infringes the rights of others (copyright, portrait rights, privacy, etc.)
  • Posting other people's personal information (name, address, contact details, photos, etc.) without their consent
  • Pretending to be someone else
  • Using the Feature for advertising or solicitation
  • Tampering with the App or the servers, or putting load on them by sending large amounts of data

4. Reporting and blocking

  • If there is a problem with someone else's star or name, let us know through "Report" in the App.
  • Stars and names of people you "Block" will no longer appear on your screen.
  • The host can remove members from the sky.
  • We check reported content, and if we decide it breaks these terms, we will delete the content, suspend that person's use, or take similar action. We respond to reports within 24 hours.

5. Storing and deleting data

  • Same Sky is stored on servers we use. For details, see the Privacy Policy.
  • A sky that no one opens any more is automatically deleted 180 days after it was last opened.
  • If the host stops the invitation, the sky disappears from the members' screens.
  • If you delete the App or change devices, you may not be able to return to a sky as the same person (a host may not be able to return as the host).

6. Changes to or suspension of the Feature

We may change the Feature, or stop it temporarily or permanently because of bugs or for safety. Whenever possible, we will give notice in advance.

7. Responsibility

  • The person who wrote content in a sky is responsible for that content.
  • We are not responsible for damage caused by the Feature stopping, content being lost, or anything that happens between users, except where we acted intentionally or with gross negligence.

8. Changes to these terms

When we change these terms, we will announce it on this page and update the "Last updated" date. Important changes will also be announced in the App.
