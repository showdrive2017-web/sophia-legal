SOPHIA: Deleting your Shared Sky data

Last updated: November 13, 2026

You can request deletion of your data stored in SOPHIA's "Shared Sky" (operator: Yoshinori Kusakari) here.

If you still have the App (the fastest way)

In the App, go to Stars list → Settings and press "Delete my Shared Sky data". Your data is deleted right away.

If you have deleted the App

Send an email to the address below with the following information.

  • Subject: "Shared Sky data deletion"
  • The name you used in the sky
  • The name of the sky you joined, or the invite code (as far as you know)
  • The name of the host (the person who invited you) (as far as you know)

Contact: showdrive2017@gmail.com

What is deleted

  • The stars you wrote (title, text, date, time) and their lines
  • Your name, colour, participation records, the record that your purchase was verified, and your user number
  • Any sky you hosted, as a whole

Please note

  • Because Shared Sky can be used without logging in, we may not be able to confirm by email alone that you are the person concerned. In that case, we may ask the host of the sky to confirm.
  • We delete the data within 30 days of receiving your request.
  • A sky you use on your own exists only on your device, so it was already erased when you deleted the App.
  • A sky that no one opens any more is automatically deleted 180 days after it was last opened.
  • Report records (such as a copy of a reported star or name) may be kept so that we can act on them.
